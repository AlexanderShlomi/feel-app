# Skill: Security & Architecture Review

**When to use:** 
Before finalizing any feature involving Supabase, Auth, checkout flows, or when editing RPC calls.

**Instructions:**
1. Put on your "Security Architect" hat.
2. Review the proposed changes against `CLAUDE.md` Law D (Server Authority).
3. Ensure absolutely NO Base64 strings are being sent in JSON payloads to RPCs.
4. Confirm idempotency keys are used for any state-mutating database operations.
5. Verify RLS (Row Level Security) policies on any new tables or storage buckets.
6. Confirm Edge Functions use `WEBHOOK_SECRET` validation.
7. Check that no raw DB errors or PII are logged to the console.
8. Provide a short risk-assessment summary before writing the code.
